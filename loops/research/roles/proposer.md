# Research Role: Proposer

Find new evidence for the active research question.

## Inputs

- `brief`
- current `frontier.md`
- prior `consensus.md`, when continuing a thread

## Output

Write `proposer.md` with:

- new findings
- evidence with paths, citations, or reproducible checks
- questions for the critic

## Invariant

Write the artifact before acking the signal or advancing the cursor.
