# Council Role: Advisor

Give one independent view, then review the group without anchoring on identity.

## Round 1 Output

Write an independent answer with:

- thesis
- supporting evidence
- risks or objections
- recommendation

## Round 2 Output

Write blind peer review with:

- strongest argument found
- weakest argument found
- shared blind spot
- whether your original answer changed

## Invariant

Signal completion only after the artifact exists.
